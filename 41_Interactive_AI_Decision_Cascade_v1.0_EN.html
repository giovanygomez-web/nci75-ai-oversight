1 · Macro signal ingestion · this month's inputs to the model Stage 1

The 9 pre-registered macroeconomic features arrive from the master panel (built by WP1). The three highlighted features (H1/H2/H3) are those with pre-registered directional hypotheses.

2 · Per-asset XGBoost inference · four independent regressors Stage 2

Each of the four assets has its own XGBoost regressor (200 trees, depth 3, learning rate 0.05) trained on the trailing 60 months. Each model ingests the same 9 features and predicts a directional signal for the next month.

Inference latency: ~30 ms for all four models combined · this is the "AI moment" — the point at which the algorithm forms its opinion about the near-term regime.

3 · SHAP attribution · why the model thinks that Stage 3

SHAP TreeExplainer decomposes each per-asset prediction into individual feature contributions. This is the interpretability layer that makes the AI's reasoning auditable and enables the H1/H2/H3 sign check.

4-5-6 · From raw signal to constrained rebalance Stages 4-6

The raw XGBoost signal is converted to a weight adjustment (Δw = signal × scale). The HOTL cap enforces the pre-registered ±10 pp bound (Stage 5). The simplex projection ensures the final weights are non-negative and sum to one (Stage 6).

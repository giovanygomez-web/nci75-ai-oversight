Where does the overlay adjust vs baseline?

The overlay proposes a weight adjustment Δwi per asset, capped at ±10 pp (HOTL bound). Positive Δ = overweight, negative Δ = underweight vs baseline.

Overlay adjustment logic

The overlay's Δw is a bounded function of the three pre-registered macro features. This month's macro state:

Verify the arithmetic

Reader check: sum the six values in the "wi,oly·ri (overlay)" column of the decomposition table above. The sum equals the value shown as Overlay rp,oly(t) in the amber box. This is the arithmetic identity rp = w · r. Every month.

Why this matters. The framework's audit trail records these six per-asset contributions per rebalance. This is what enables ex-post reconstruction of any month's decision (§C.9 of the Configuration Manual).
